## Design Context

### Users
Startup founders at pre-seed to Series A stage. They're time-poor, skeptical of bloated agencies, and need a credible visual presence fast to raise funding or close early customers. They browse on desktop and mobile, often from a referral or outreach link.

### Brand Personality
Precise · Agile · Credible. The studio positions itself as the anti-agency: no overhead, no juniors, no surprise invoices. The tone is a smart partner talking to another partner — direct, honest, professional without stiffness.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode. Clean, contemporary, founder-credible. White surfaces with dark slate typography. Teal (#0f766e) accent. Portfolio-first — the hero visual is a scrolling gallery of real client work. Feels like a well-made product, not a design agency showreel. References: Linear, Vercel, Stripe (quality bar), not Behance or Dribbble.

### Design Principles
1. **Work speaks first** — real portfolio imagery is the hero; nothing should upstage it
2. **Founders scan, not read** — hierarchy must work at a glance; no decorative copy
3. **Credibility through restraint** — every section earns its space; cut before adding
4. **Fast and intentional** — motion exists to confirm state changes, not to impress
5. **One system** — identity, deck, and web language must feel like one studio made them all
