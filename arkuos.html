Arkuos

Nonprofit Education Identity Campaign Social impact

Arkuos is a socio-educational action space. We built a visual identity around its warm symbol: yellow, orange, and red strokes that meet in a clear centre, black wordmark, and a tagline that states the mission in one line. Deliverables include the full logo lockup, the geometric mark, campaign art direction, and digital-ready assets aligned with the public site.
